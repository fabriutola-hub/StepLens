## Becoming a maintainer

We're a small project; the bar for adding maintainers is "demonstrated, sustained
contribution + clearly aligned values." If you've been opening quality PRs for
a few months and want to do more, open a Discussion or ping an existing
maintainer.

## Stepping down

Maintainers are welcome to step down at any time — open a PR removing yourself
from this list, or ping another maintainer to do it. No drama.
